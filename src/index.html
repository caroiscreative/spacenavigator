    Density + Satellites: open the density map and click an altitude band to highlight every satellite in that orbital shell.

    Outer + Deep: zoom to the outer solar system and enable interstellar visitors to see the hyperbolic trajectories of ʻOumuamua, Borisov and 3I/ATLAS.
    G+]
    Risk + Time: open the conjunction panel, fly to an event and speed up time with ] to watch the approach in fast-forward.
    Iclick
    Explorer + Group: open the Explorer, select a satellite group (e.g. Starlink) and click to see all trajectories at once.
    ABOUT CREATOR
    WHY IT EXISTS

    SpaceNavigator started with a simple question: what is happening up there, right now? The data exists — over 27,000 catalogued objects in real time — but most tools that visualize it are built for engineers, not for exploration.

    I wanted something anyone could open and be left speechless. Something that feels as alive as the space it represents.
